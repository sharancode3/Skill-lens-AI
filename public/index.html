Candidate Evaluation Rules & Guidelines

Please read these evaluation standards and anti-cheating proctoring policies carefully before beginning your session for Candidate.

1. What This Interview Is

This is an adaptive, multi-turn technical evaluation conducted by an AI interviewer based on your actual completed course curriculum. The interview continuously evaluates your responses, dynamically adjusting technical difficulty and topic progression based on your demonstrated technical depth.

2. How to Answer

Your evaluation score depends on clear technical communication. Follow these core answering guidelines:

  • Be Genuine & Specific: Explain the exact mechanisms, architecture, data structures, or trade-offs involved in your engineering choices.
  • Avoid Vague Filler: Surface-level answers like "it was good" or "used python" trigger mandatory technical follow-ups.
  • Refusal & Conduct Penalties: Intentionally dodging questions ("idk"), off-topic inputs, or dismissive responses ("do as u like") register conduct warnings and can cause immediate session suspension.

3. Hallucination & Fact-Checking Policy

The AI interviewer actively verifies your claims against authoritative technical standards. Inventing non-existent APIs, claiming incorrect performance metrics, or fabricating library methods registers a Hallucination Flag, deducts points, and triggers immediate correction.

4. Anti-Cheating & Proctoring Rules

1
Mandatory Fullscreen Mode: You must remain in Fullscreen mode throughout the entire session. Exiting fullscreen triggers a warning and auto-recovery attempt. Reaching 4 exits results in immediate suspension.
2
Tab & Window Switching: Navigating away from this browser window or switching tabs at any point triggers a proctoring violation. Tab switching immediately terminates the session on the 1st occurrence.
3
Copy/Paste & Shortcut Lockdown: Copying, pasting, cutting text, or using developer shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, F12) is disabled. Attempting copy/paste triggers a warning on the 1st attempt and suspension on the 2nd attempt.
4
Suspension & 5-Minute Cooldown: Suspended sessions are permanently recorded as a FAILED / SUSPENDED evaluation. A 5-minute cooldown period is strictly enforced before candidate re-attempts are permitted.

5. Post-Interview Report & Panel Verdict

Upon completing all required curriculum topics, your session concludes automatically. You will receive a structured Interview Panel Verdict detailing key strengths, identified technical gaps, response speed analytics, and actionable recommended next steps.

Camera Setup & Proctoring Consent

To ensure evaluation integrity, this technical interview utilizes automated camera-based proctoring. By enabling your camera, you consent to continuous real-time monitoring of:

  • Face Presence: Verifies the candidate remains in frame.
  • Attention & Gaze Tracking: Identifies if candidate is looking away for prolonged periods.
  • Device Detection: Monitors for prohibited cell phone usage during technical rounds.
Camera Access Required: You cannot proceed with the technical evaluation unproctored. Please click the enable button and grant browser camera permissions.
